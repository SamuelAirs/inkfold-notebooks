# Support

Inkfold keeps your notebooks on a wooden bookshelf: covers you decorate, paper you choose, pages that turn. Here's how it works, and how to reach me when something doesn't.

## Getting started

- **A new notebook:** tap **+** on the shelf for the Bookshop, then tap a journal on its first shelf > *Take Home and Open* (or *Take Home*: it's on your shelf when you leave). To name it and choose its cover, lettering, paper and typing font, touch and hold it on your shelf > *Decorate*.
- **Opening one:** tap a book. To go back to the shelf, tap the back button at the top left.
- **Moving or decorating one:** touch and hold a book, then drag it to any shelf. Hold and let go without moving for its menu: **Open**, **Peek**, **Decorate**, **Share** (as a PDF or an Inkfold file), **Lock with Face ID**, **Move to…** and **Delete**. To lay a book flat or stand it up, turn it with two fingers, as if turning a dial.
- **The shelf's ••• menu:** *Arrange* (*Tidy Up* to put them straight, *Stand Every Book Up*, *Sort Books*, your saved layouts, *Shelves* and *Physics*), *Bookcases*, *Recently Deleted*, *iCloud Drive Sync*, *Unlimited Notebooks* and *Restore Purchase*, *Settings…*, and *Help* (this page, the privacy policy and *Share Diagnostics Log…*). Touch and hold the page dots at the foot of the shelf to rename the bookcase.
- **Bigger books on an iPad:** *Arrange* > *Shelves* sets how many shelves a page shows (on iPads and iPhones other than the Duo, whose bookcase is made to its screens). Fewer shelves, bigger books; every book stays where it was.
- **Decorating:** *Decorate* puts the book itself on the wood. Tap its cover, title, spine or edge for their choices, and swing the cover open for the endpapers and the paper. Your Pencil draws on the cover, and on the spine when the book turns to it, with the pens beside the book: the pen, pencil and marker (tap one again for its kinds and sizes), the eraser (tap it again for *Pixels*, which rubs out only what it passes over, or *Whole Strokes*, and its sizes), the size, *Finger* to draw with a finger, the colour ring for any colour (or gold, silver, white, black, oxblood or navy), *Undo* and *Clear*; *Write by Hand…* on the spine's card writes its label. Drag stickers off the sheet; under it, *Photos* makes a sticker of a photo's subject (cut out on your iPhone or iPad, as Photos lifts one), *Paste* takes a picture you copied, and *Stickers* brings your stickers from the keyboard (the ones you made in Photos, Memoji and Genmoji) or an emoji; with a keyboard attached, press the globe key for them.
- **Shelf labels:** touch and hold a shelf's edge to label it: a brass plaque, a label holder or a chalk label (or, in the Bookshop, the Directory knob > *Shelf Labels and Wood…* > *Shelf Labels*, then tap the shelf it's for). Drag a label along its shelf or onto another; hold it at the top or the foot of the screen for the next page of shelves, or at a side for another bookcase. Tap a label to rename it; touch and hold it to change its style, move it to another bookcase or take it off.
- **Book stands:** put a book on a stand, touch and hold the stand for its menu, and choose *Follow My Place*: each time you come back to the shelf, it turns to the page the book was last open at.

## Writing in a notebook

There are no modes to switch:

- **Drawing:** an Apple Pencil always draws, and so does your finger while *Draw with Finger* is on (it's off at first). Every Apple pen and pencil, the erasers, the lasso and the ruler are in the palette (down the sides on an iPad; ••• > *Settings* > *Tools* puts it along the bottom instead).
- **Typing:** just start typing on a keyboard, or tap the keyboard button among the controls for the keyboard on the screen. The words go on the ruled lines of the page you're on and run on from page to page; a new page is added when you reach the end. With *Draw with Finger* off, tap a line to type there. Tap the keyboard button again, or press Escape, to put the typing away. Your drawing stays on top of the typing.
- **Select:** the arrow after the lasso among the tools. With it, your Pencil or your finger (whatever *Draw with Finger* says) taps a line to put the typing there, and the keyboard comes up; taps a photo, a sticker or a sticky note to pick it up, then drag it, drag its corner to make it bigger or smaller, or turn it with two fingers; taps washi tape to bring its ends' handles up. Drag the knob above the one you picked up to turn it (it settles square at each quarter turn); with an Apple Pencil Pro, hold the Pencil just above it and roll the Pencil to turn it the same way. Tap the one you picked up, or touch and hold anything, for its menu; tap elsewhere to put it down; touch and hold bare paper to paste. The lasso picks out ink, and its menu's *Make a Sticker* cuts the ink out as a sticker (kept in *Your Stickers* too, for any page); *Make a Stamp* keeps the ink as a stamp of your own, first among the stamps, to press in any colour. The keyboard button only shows and hides the keyboard. With a trackpad or a mouse, a photo, sticker or note shows a thin outline as the pointer passes over it.
- **Your pens:** tap a pen you're already using for its own menu: its kind, its width, *Stabilisation* (the line steadied as you write: it follows the Pencil on a short string, so a shaky hand comes out smooth, and catches up to where you lift; a little for handwriting, more for long smooth lines), *Pressure* (from even to twice as much), *Ends* (thin, like a brush's, or thick where the pen goes down and lifts, as the pen comes) and *Save This Pen*. Your saved pens come first in the colours beside the tools; tap one to take it in hand, touch and hold it to delete it. Tilt a pencil or a crayon onto its side to shade.
- **Shapes:** draw a line, a circle or an oval, a triangle, a square or rectangle, a five-pointed star (tip to tip, or round its outline) or a zigzag, and hold the Pencil still at the end: it becomes exact.
- **Fill:** drag the colour ring, or a colour beside it, into a shape you've drawn closed and let go: the shape fills with that colour. A small gap in the line still holds it; drop another colour in to change it. A fill is ink: erase it, lasso it, undo it.
- **Scratch to erase:** with a pen, scrub quickly back and forth over ink and it's rubbed away (Undo brings it back). On bare paper a zigzag is just ink, so shading stays. Turn it off with *Scratch to Erase* in the pen's menu.
- **Stamps:** the stamp after Select among the tools. Tap it, then tap the page to press the stamp in the colour chosen; tap it again for the stamps and their size, as rubber stamps come: ⅝ inch, 1 inch or 1½ inches on the page. There are over a hundred, in families: shapes; to-do marks and words (a checkbox, a priority flag, DONE, URGENT, PAID); home and its chores (laundry, dishes, trash and recycling, the yard, tools, the baby); errands (the shopping cart, the car, mail, bills, the doctor and the dentist); activities; media (a movie, a TV show, books, an album, a podcast, a video game, a board game, WATCHED, READ, PLAYED); faces, weather, and today's date or the time, from your device's own clock. Your own stamps, made with the lasso's *Make a Stamp*, come first and sync with your library; touch and hold one to delete it. The row of little pictures above them jumps to each family. A stamp is ink: erase it, lasso it, undo it. On an iPad, the Pencil held just above the page shows the stamp's outline where it will press; with an Apple Pencil Pro, roll the Pencil to turn the stamp (it rests upright and at every 45 degrees, with a tick).
- **Write as Text (iPad):** after the stamp. The Pencil writes on the lines and iPadOS turns it into typed text as you write (held just above the lines, the Pencil shows a faint cursor where the words will go); scratch out typed words to delete them, circle them to pick them out, or touch and hold between them for a space.
- **Convert to Text:** lasso your handwriting and choose *Convert to Text*: each handwritten line becomes a typed line, with its bullets, indents and the blank lines between.
- **Text size:** the size key while typing, or *Format* > *Size* over words picked out: a step larger or smaller, *Heading*, *Subheading*, *Normal*, *Small*, *Tiny*, or any size from 8 to 72 points. Words larger than a line take as many lines as they need.

More, where each belongs:

- **Draw with Finger:** the finger beside the tools. Lit, your finger draws; off, your finger turns pages and only the Pencil draws. Unless you've chosen yourself, Inkfold turns it off the first time it sees an Apple Pencil.
- **Adding:** the plus on the blank page after the last one, or the **+** beside the pages: a page, a page from a template, a drawing page (one the typing skips), a photo, a voice memo, a sticker, a sticky note or washi tape. A tap on the blank page itself adds a page straight away.
- **A page's own menu:** tap its number at the foot. Its paper, its tab, *Zoom In*, *Full Screen* and *Delete Page*.
- **Zoom:** pinch a page to look closer and write small and neat: the pages grow where they lie, up to four times, and everything works as before. Move about with two fingers (or one, where it wouldn't draw or type). Pinch in, double-tap, or tap the badge in the corner to come back to the book's size; turning the page comes back first. On a keyboard, Command-= and Command-− step in and out, and Command-0 comes back.
- **The library card:** turn back from page 1 to the inside of the cover, and tap the bookplate or the card in its pocket. Sign it on its lines with your Pencil or a finger: each line signed is stamped with the day.
- **Washi tape:** tap its roll among the tools, then tap where a strip starts and again where it ends, or drag from one to the other; drag an end of the piece just laid to put it exactly. Tap the roll again for its patterns, width, colours and how sheer it lies, or *Draw a Tape…* to make your own.
- **Photos:** a photo can be dragged anywhere, across the spine too. Washi tape over it holds it down; touch and hold the photo for *Peel Off Tape* to move it again. A new photo lies on top of everything on the page, as on a desk. Draw on it and your ink lies over the photo from the first touch of the pen, with any pen; the ink you drew before the photo stays under it. Move the photo and the ink on it stays where you wrote it, on the page. Its menu's *Send to Paper* lays it under the drawing, *Bring Above Ink* back over.
- **Stickers:** **+** > *Sticker…* for the sticker sheet: the fourteen stickers the covers have (write on the paper label), *From Photos…* for any picture, or *Paste*. A picture with a clear background, such as a subject you lifted out of a photo and copied, becomes a sticker cut round its shape with a white edge: in Photos, touch and hold a subject to lift it, tap *Copy*, then *Paste as Sticker* in Inkfold (or ⌘V on a keyboard). Stickers move, turn and resize like photos, lie across the spine, and tape holds them down.
- **Sticky notes:** **+** > *Sticky Note* in yellow, four pastels or clear film. A note lies over your drawing, as a real one would. Write on it with the Pencil, or tap it to type on it; its words and ink go wherever it goes. Touch and hold it for *Move to Page…* (to carry a task on to another day), its colour, its *Shape* (square, rounded, tall, wide, circle, oval, a speech bubble, a heart, a star, a cloud, a gift tag, a ticket or a banner, each shown as a picture), its *Edge* (straight, torn top, scalloped or zigzag), its *Border* (none, thin or wide, in white or a colour of yours), or *Delete*, which peels it off.
- **Photos' shapes, edges and borders:** touch and hold a photo for *Shape*, its outline (as taken, square, rounded, circle, oval, heart, star, arch, ticket or gift tag); *Edge*, how that outline is cut (straight, deckled, scalloped, zigzag or perforated); and *Border* (none, thin, wide, a card or a polaroid, in white or a colour of yours). *Adjust Crop…* moves the picture inside its shape: drag it, pinch to make it bigger, then *Done*. A picture sticker's *Border* is white, thick, holographic or none. They print in a PDF as they look on the page.
- **Your stickers:** stickers you make of ink are kept in *Your Stickers*, in a row on the sticker sheet (**+** > *Sticker…*); touch and hold any picture sticker for *Add to Your Stickers*. Tap one to put it on a page, touch and hold it to remove it. They sync with your library.
- **Arranging:** touch and hold a photo, sticker or note for *Arrange* (*Bring to Front*, *Bring Forward*, *Send Backward*, *Send to Back*), *Bring Above Ink* or *Send to Paper*, and *Glue Down*, which holds it as tape does. For ink, lasso it: the lasso's menu has *Arrange* too (*Bring to Front*, *Bring Forward*, *Send Backward*, *Send to Back*), so *Send Backward* puts the ink you picked under the photo beneath it, and *Bring to Front* brings it back over everything.
- **On the Page:** anything on a page can lie over or under anything else, with no limit. The ink you draw now lies over everything on the page, and what you put down (a photo, sticker, note or strip of tape) lands on top, so ink drawn before a photo stays under it and ink drawn after lies over it. There's no layer to choose. The layers button opens *On the Page*, one list of everything on the page, top first: your ink, the typed text, photos, stickers, notes, memos and loose tape. Drag any row to put it in another place, a photo down between two inks, say. Tap an ink row for its opacity; its eye hides it. **+** adds a layer of your own on top, with a name, so you can hide a sketch later. The eraser rubs out the topmost ink under it, wherever it lies, and writing across the spine lies over a photo on both pages.
- **Sounds:** ••• > *Settings* > *Sounds* turns off every sound: pages, the shelf, tape and stickers.
- **Undo:** two fingers tap to undo, three to redo: drawing, washi tape, and photos, stickers, sticky notes and voice memos added, moved, resized, written on or deleted. On an iPhone, where the strip has no room for redo, touch and hold the undo button for it.
- **The ••• menu:** *Share* (this page or the whole notebook as a PDF, to Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files, print it, or send it as an Inkfold file; typed text stays real text in the PDF), *Pages* (one or two side by side), *Decorate* and *Read Beside*.

To turn pages, use the arrows at the bottom, swipe with two fingers, or (with *Draw with Finger* off) swipe or tap near the page's edge. With a keyboard, the right and left arrow keys turn pages while you aren't typing, and Page Down and Page Up always do.

## On the iPhone Duo

- **Opened like a book:** two pages side by side, with the spine on the hinge, and a board down the middle of the bookcase so no book stands on the fold.
- **Half folded like a laptop:** the page stands on the upper half, and the keyboard and the tools lie on the lower. The page scrolls to keep your typing in sight; scroll it yourself with two fingers.
- **Closed:** on the outer display, **+** > *Pocket Note…* takes a quick note with your finger and files it on today's page of your Journal.
- An Apple Pencil draws on the iPhone Duo as it does on an iPad. Without one, light *Draw with Finger* (the finger beside the tools) and your finger draws.

## Search

The search field under *Library* finds words in every notebook: titles, typed text, the words typed on sticky notes, and handwriting. Tap a result to open the notebook at that page.

Handwriting is read on your device a moment after you stop writing. Neat writing is read best, and some languages and very small or joined-up writing may not be read at all; search finds only what was read.

## Reading books

Inkfold reads books you have as files: **EPUB** (without copy protection), **PDF**, plain **text** and comics as **CBZ** (a CBR packed with RAR can't be opened: Calibre or a zip tool makes a CBZ of it).

- **Adding one:** tap **+** for the Bookshop, then the label of its shelf *Your Own Books* > *Files*, and choose it (several at once is fine); or share the file from another app and choose Inkfold. Inkfold keeps its own copy; the original stays where it was. *Your Own Books* also has *Public Domain* (free classics), *Catalog* (your own OPDS server, such as Calibre-Web) and *Folder* (every book in a folder at once), and the Bookshop's own shelves hold the same classics to take home.
- **Reading:** tap the book. Swipe or tap near the page's edge to turn it; on a keyboard, the arrow keys and the space bar. Two pages show side by side on the iPad, on a phone turned sideways, and on the iPhone Duo opened like a book.
- **The ••• menu:** *Contents* (when the book has a table of contents), *Text* (font, size and paper, for EPUB and text; a PDF keeps its own pages) and *Pages* (one or two).
- Inkfold remembers your place in each book. Books are free: the 3-notebook limit doesn't count them.
- **Highlights:** touch and hold a word to mark it, and drag on to choose more. Let go for the bar: a colour highlights the words, or *Look Up*, *Add to Notes* and *Copy*. Tap a highlight for the same bar, to change its colour or *Delete* it. With the Apple Pencil, the highlighter along a line highlights it too.
- Not yet: books with copy protection (DRM) from Apple Books or Kindle, and searching inside a book.

## Unlimited Notebooks

Inkfold is free with up to 3 notebooks of your own on the shelf (the sample notebooks it starts with don't count), with every cover, paper and pen. **Unlimited Notebooks** is one purchase of $4.99 (in US dollars; the App Store shows your own currency), with no subscription: once bought, you can add as many notebooks as you like.

- **Buying it:** take a journal home from the Bookshop (**+**) when you have 3 notebooks of your own, or choose *Unlimited Notebooks…* in the shelf's **•••** menu, then *Unlock*. Apple's purchase sheet takes the payment.
- **On another iPhone or iPad,** or after reinstalling: the shelf's **•••** menu > *Restore Purchase*, signed in with the same Apple Account.
- **Ask to Buy:** if a parent approves later, Inkfold unlocks by itself.
- Your notebooks are never locked away: without the purchase you can still open, write in, share and delete every notebook you have. Only making a fourth of your own waits for it.

## Trying the samples

In the shelf's **•••** menu, *Settings…* > **Sample Library** fills the shelf with sample notebooks and books at once. Your own notebooks are kept apart, exactly as they are, and come back the moment you turn it off. (The same switch is in the iPhone's Settings app, under Inkfold, read the next time Inkfold opens.)

## Questions

- **Where are my notebooks kept?** In the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. They're included in your device's backups (iCloud Backup or your computer), which is also how they move to a new device. To keep one library on your iPhone and your iPad, the shelf's ••• menu > *Sync with iCloud Drive…* copies it into a folder you pick in your own iCloud Drive.
- **Can I get a deleted notebook back?** Yes, for 30 days. A book you delete goes to *Recently Deleted* in the shelf's **•••** menu: swipe right on it (or tap it) to recover it, and it goes back on the shelf. Right after deleting, the message at the foot of the screen has an *Undo* too. After 30 days, or when you choose *Delete Now* there, it's gone for good. Deleting a notebook locked with Face ID asks for Face ID first.
- **My finger turns pages instead of drawing.** Tap the finger beside the tools in the notebook so it lights up (*Draw with Finger*).
- **The books won't stay still.** In the shelf's **•••** menu, *Arrange*: turn off *Physics*, then choose *Tidy Up*.
