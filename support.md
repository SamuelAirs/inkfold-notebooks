# Support

Inkfold keeps your notebooks on a wooden bookshelf: covers you decorate, paper you choose, pages that turn. Here's how it works, and how to reach me when something doesn't.

## Getting started

- **A new notebook:** tap **+** on the shelf > *New Notebook*. It drops onto a shelf and opens its *New Notebook* sheet: name it, then choose its cover, lettering, paper and typing font.
- **Opening one:** tap a book. To go back to the shelf, tap the back button at the top left.
- **Moving or decorating one:** touch and hold a book, then drag it to any shelf. Hold and let go without moving for its menu: **Open**, **Decorate**, **Share as PDF**, **Delete**.
- **The shelf's ••• menu:** *Unlimited Notebooks* and *Restore Purchase*, the wood (oak, walnut, ash), *Tilt to Move Books*, *Tidy Up* to stand them up straight, and *Help* (this page and the privacy policy).

## Writing in a notebook

The three buttons at the top of an open notebook choose what your finger does:

- **Pen:** draw and write with every Apple pen and pencil, the erasers, the lasso and the ruler, in the palette along the bottom. An Apple Pencil always draws, in any mode.
- **Type:** type on the ruled lines. Your text runs on from page to page, and a new page is added when you reach the end.
- **Text boxes:** tap anywhere on a page for a box of text. Drag it to move it, widen it from its right edge, or make it a yellow sticky note.

More, in the notebook's **•••** menu:

- **Zoom In to Draw:** the page fills the screen, pinch to zoom in up to 8 times, then tap *Done*.
- **Draw with Finger:** on, your finger draws; off, your finger turns pages and only the Pencil draws. Unless you've chosen yourself, Inkfold turns it off the first time it sees an Apple Pencil.
- **Pages:** one page or two side by side; *Add Page*, *Add Drawing Page* (one the typing skips) and *Delete Page*.
- **This Page's Paper:** a different design for one page.
- **Share as PDF:** this page (or the two you see) or the whole notebook, to Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files or a printer. Typed text stays real text in the PDF.

To turn pages, use the arrows at the bottom, swipe with two fingers, or (in Type mode, or with *Draw with Finger* off) swipe or tap near the page's edge.

## On the iPhone Duo

- **Opened like a book:** two pages side by side, with the spine on the hinge, and a board down the middle of the bookcase so no book stands on the fold.
- **Half folded like a laptop:** one page above the fold and one below, turning upward like a reporter's pad, so the lower page lies flat under your pen.
- The iPhone Duo doesn't support Apple Pencil yet, so your finger draws there.

## Search

The search field under *Library* finds words in every notebook: titles, typed text, text boxes and handwriting. Tap a result to open the notebook at that page.

Handwriting is read on your device a moment after you stop writing. Neat writing is read best, and some languages and very small or joined-up writing may not be read at all; search finds only what was read.

## Reading books

Inkfold reads books you have as files: **EPUB** (without copy protection), **PDF** and plain **text**.

- **Adding one:** tap **+** > *Add a Book…* and choose it in Files (several at once is fine), or share the file from another app and choose Inkfold. Inkfold keeps its own copy; the original stays where it was.
- **Reading:** tap the book. Swipe or tap near the page's edge to turn it; on a keyboard, the arrow keys and the space bar. Two pages show side by side on the iPad, on a phone turned sideways, and on the iPhone Duo opened like a book.
- **The ••• menu:** *Contents* (when the book has a table of contents), *Text* (font, size and paper, for EPUB and text; a PDF keeps its own pages) and *Pages* (one or two).
- Inkfold remembers your place in each book. Books are free: the 3-notebook limit doesn't count them.
- Not yet: books with copy protection (DRM) from Apple Books or Kindle, pictures inside EPUBs, highlights and notes in books, and searching inside a book.

## Unlimited Notebooks

Inkfold is free with up to 3 notebooks of your own on the shelf (the sample notebooks it starts with don't count), with every cover, paper and pen. **Unlimited Notebooks** is one purchase of $4.99 (in US dollars; the App Store shows your own currency), with no subscription: once bought, you can add as many notebooks as you like.

- **Buying it:** tap **+** > *New Notebook* when you have 3 notebooks of your own, or choose *Unlimited Notebooks…* in the shelf's **•••** menu, then *Unlock*. Apple's purchase sheet takes the payment.
- **On another iPhone or iPad,** or after reinstalling: the shelf's **•••** menu > *Restore Purchase*, signed in with the same Apple Account.
- **Ask to Buy:** if a parent approves later, Inkfold unlocks by itself.
- Your notebooks are never locked away: without the purchase you can still open, write in, share and delete every notebook you have. Only making a fourth of your own waits for it.

## Trying the samples

**Settings > Inkfold > Sample Library** fills the shelf with thirteen sample notebooks the next time Inkfold opens (close it from the app switcher first). Your own notebooks are kept apart and come back when you turn it off.

## Questions

- **Where are my notebooks kept?** On this iPhone or iPad only, in the app's own storage. They're included in your device's backups (iCloud Backup or your computer), which is also how they move to a new device. Inkfold 1.0 doesn't sync between devices.
- **Can I get a deleted notebook back?** No: deleting a notebook can't be undone. To keep a copy, share it as a PDF first.
- **My finger turns pages instead of drawing.** Open the notebook's **•••** menu and turn on *Draw with Finger*.
- **The books won't stay still.** Turn off *Tilt to Move Books* in the shelf's **•••** menu, then choose *Tidy Up*.
