# Privacy policy

**Inkfold Notebooks** for iPhone and iPad<br>
Effective October 31, 2026

<p class="lead">The short version: Inkfold collects nothing about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and there are no Inkfold servers. The app's only connection is to Apple's App Store, for the Unlimited Notebooks purchase. Your notebooks stay on your device.</p>

## What stays on your device

- **Your notebooks:** their covers, pages, ink, typed text, text boxes and sticky notes, kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad.
- **Your settings:** finger drawing, the shelf's tilt and wood, the pens you last chose, and whether Unlimited Notebooks was bought on this device's Apple Account.
- **Handwriting, read for search:** Inkfold reads your handwriting as text with Apple's text recognition, which runs on the device. The text is kept with the page it came from, so search can find it, and goes nowhere else.

Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer) include the app's notebooks, as they do for any app. Those backups are Apple's, under your settings.

## The purchase

Unlimited Notebooks is bought through Apple's App Store, with Apple's own purchase sheet. Apple handles the payment; Inkfold never sees your payment details, your Apple Account or your name. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account bought it, and its price in your currency. Apple's privacy policy covers the App Store.

## What leaves your device

Only what you send yourself. When you share a page or a notebook as a PDF, the share sheet sends it where you choose: Mail, Messages, AirDrop, the Files app or a printer. Inkfold doesn't send anything by itself.

## Permissions

Inkfold doesn't ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. The *Support* and *Privacy Policy* links in the shelf's ••• menu open these pages in Safari. The bookshelf reads the device's tilt (its motion sensors) to lean the books, which needs no permission and never leaves the app. You can turn it off in the shelf's ••• menu.

## Children

Inkfold isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect anything from anyone.

## Crash reports

If you've chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass on crash reports, which hold no notebook content. That's Apple's setting, and it's yours to change.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
